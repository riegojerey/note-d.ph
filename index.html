Our Story

Two Accountants, One Love

Note.d began with two accountants who shared everything — a desk, a dream, and a love for the way a good perfume could transform an ordinary moment into something unforgettable. But walking past the designer counters, they realized the scents they adored came with numbers that didn't add up. So they did what any good accountant would: they found another way.

Armed with research, raw oils, and the kind of patience only balance sheets can teach, they began mixing their first bottles on their kitchen counter. Trial after trial, night after night, they chased something that smelled like the luxury they couldn't afford — and found something even better. Something their own. Every Note.d fragrance is the result of two people who refused to let a price tag stand between them and the scents they loved. Bottled by hand, priced with heart, sent out hoping it finds someone just like them.
