Why perfume oil

It lasts longer

Most perfumes are mostly alcohol and water, which evaporate quickly. Ours is pure oil, so the scent stays on your skin through the day.

It costs a fraction

A designer bottle can cost ₱5,000 or more. Our 30mL is ₱275, inspired by the same scent you already love.

You can try it small

Start with a 10mL for ₱119. When you find your signature, the 30mL gives you three times as much.

Questions

Are these the original designer perfumes?

No. Note.d perfumes are our own oil-based blends inspired by famous designer scents, made to capture the same character at a fraction of the price. We're not affiliated with any of the brands we mention.

How do I apply perfume oil?

Dab or roll it onto your pulse points: wrists, neck and behind the ears. A little goes a long way. Let it settle instead of rubbing your wrists together.

Which size should I get?

If you're trying a scent for the first time, get the 10mL for ₱119. If you already love it, the 30mL for ₱275 is better value.

How much is delivery?
